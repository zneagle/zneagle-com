01Why this company

Synthium generates human-robot-interaction training data for humanoid robots through interactive simulations, converting live human-robot interactions into labeled, multimodal datasets at a claimed fraction of the cost of teleoperation [company]. It partners with Unitree, A*STAR, Google for Startups, and MassRobotics [company] — a simulation/data platform rather than a hardware-deployment company.

02Where their work intersects my trajectory

  • Sensors — PLC-monitored, sensor-driven systems at Level99.
  • Signal-path reasoning — audio-engineering systems model.
  • Software / AI systems reasoning — current study direction.

Synthium's product is squarely data/simulation infrastructure, not hardware I can point to direct build or repair experience against. A careers page exists [company] but I found no specific listing. My honest overlap is narrower here than at the hardware-facing companies on this list: sensor-and-interactive-system exposure that's at least adjacent to understanding what a human-robot-interaction capture setup involves.

I have no simulation-platform, human-robot-interaction research, or data-pipeline experience, and I'm not presenting myself as a fit for Synthium's core technical roles. This dossier is acknowledgment of an interesting company with a genuinely thin honest overlap, not a request for a specific role.

Synthium letter, 2026-09-18. Acknowledgment of interest — the honest overlap here is narrow.

Dear Synthium Team,

I want to be upfront that Synthium's work — generating human-robot-interaction training data through simulation — is further from my direct experience than most companies I'm writing to. I don't have simulation-platform or HRI-research experience.

What I can offer is adjacent: sensor and PLC-monitored interactive-systems work at a venue where people and machines shared the same space, and a signal-path way of thinking from audio engineering that at least helps me understand what a capture pipeline is doing conceptually.

I don't have a specific role in mind. If a conversation at RoboBoston is useful despite the narrow overlap, I'd welcome it. Thank you for considering this.

Sincerely,
