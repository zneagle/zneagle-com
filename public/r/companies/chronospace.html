01Why this company

ChronoSpace is building a "Geometry-Native Foundation Model" meant to let AI reason about space, time, and motion directly, rather than only from flat images — aimed at manufacturing, robotics, and entertainment [company]. It's an early-stage, a16z Speedrun-backed team, which means the work is almost certainly concentrated in senior research and modeling roles rather than build/operations — but capturing the physical world in 4D in the first place is itself a hardware and rigging problem, which is the part I can speak to honestly.

04Individualized letter

ChronoSpace letter, 2026-09-18. Speculative interest — I found no public opening to respond to.

Read the full letter

Dear ChronoSpace Team,

I want to be upfront: I couldn't find a public job listing at ChronoSpace, and what you're building — a geometry-native foundation model — is squarely research-scientist territory I'm not claiming to be ready for. I'm writing anyway because the problem underneath the model is one I recognize: before anything can reason about space, time, and motion, someone has to actually capture that data cleanly, which is a rigging, sensor, and signal-path problem as much as a modeling one.

My background is audio engineering, sensor and PLC-monitored systems work at an interactive venue, and multi-state technical/scenic installation — including building a clean-room structure for PCB manufacturing at a Starlink facility. All of it comes down to the same skill: getting a physical capture or control system to behave correctly and consistently, under real constraints.

I don't know if ChronoSpace has, or needs, anything like a capture-operations or hardware-support function at this stage — you may simply be a small modeling team where that doesn't apply yet. If it does exist, or comes to exist, I'd be interested in a conversation. Thank you for considering this, and for whatever time we get at RoboBoston.

Sincerely,

Zachary Neagle
